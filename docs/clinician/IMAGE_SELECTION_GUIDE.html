Clinician Image Selection Guide

Select only the image that the hospital or authorized operator has staged for review. The Workbench does not browse or sample directly from hospital storage.

Before opening a case

  • Use an approved color fundus photograph or supported single-frame ophthalmic DICOM.
  • Do not include names, hospital numbers, or other patient-identifying information in a public or synthetic workspace.
  • Confirm that the image is the intended eye and belongs to the intended pseudonymous case.

During Confirm Image

  • Review the proposed patient key and laterality.
  • Correct the context when it is known; choose Unknown when it cannot be confirmed.
  • Continue only when the source and case context are suitable for review.
Clinical boundary: an unclear or ungradable image is not evidence of a normal eye. Record the appropriate review outcome and follow the local clinical pathway.

Sampling responsibility

The team monitors Refer/Not-refer coverage, sex, and laterality. Clinicians do not manage sampling quotas during routine image selection.

Source of truth: docs/clinician/sampling/ and the clinician manual. This guide describes the current research POC and does not replace hospital policy.
