# MONAI Decision

Status: `MONAI_EVALUATED_DEFERRED`.

MONAI is recorded as an engineering framework and Bundle/reproducibility
candidate, not as a UWF diabetic-retinopathy model. No MONAI dependency was
added, no model bundle was loaded, and the accepted clinician UI was not
replaced. Adoption remains deferred until a qualified model, exact
preprocessing equivalence, runtime/security profile, and deployment benefit
are demonstrated without changing original-pixel or provenance contracts.
