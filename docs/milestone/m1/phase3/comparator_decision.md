# Research Comparator Decisions

## PRISM

The repository PRISM adapter remains a separate CFP-domain comparator. Its
ROI cropper, transforms, five-fold loading, and fusion are not silently mixed
with the historical research-package adapter. Empty proposals remain model
output, never a reviewed-negative finding. UWF use remains
`NOT_VALIDATED_FOR_UWF` and `COMPARATOR_ONLY`.

## Native UWF lesion localization

Status: `DEFERRED_NO_QUALIFIED_CANDIDATE`. No native UWF localizer was present
with sufficient qualification evidence, so no replacement detector was
invented.

## Clef / Clef-Flash

Status: `CLEF_COMPARATOR_DEFERRED_RESOURCE_LIMIT`. No zero-shot run was
performed in this environment. Clef is not exposed in clinician flow and no
fine-tuning or clinical claim is authorized.

## Longitudinal

Status: `DISABLED`. Manual same-eye comparison may remain subject to existing
identity/chronology contracts; no learned change or risk claim is introduced.
