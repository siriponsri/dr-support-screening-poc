What should I choose?

Useใช้ได้

  • A colour fundus / retinal photograph already classed Refer or Not-refer.
  • Enough retina is visible for normal reading: optic disc, macula, vessels.
  • The original file, staged through the approved hospital process.
Screenshot / other

What should I avoid?

Skipไม่ต้องส่ง

  • Not a retinal photo: OCT, external-eye or other images.
  • Screenshots, crops, edited or annotated copies.
  • Corrupt or unreadable files, or clearly unusable images.
  • An image you know you already staged.
Blurred / dark

What if I am unsure?

Review firstไม่แน่ใจ ดูอีกครั้ง

  • Borderline blur or exposure, unsure which eye, or unsure enough retina shows: take a second look.
  • Would you still use it clinically? Stage it. If not, skip it or ask the coordinator.
  • Do not guess or invent details. “Unknown” is fine.
No

Do I need to manage dataset balance myself?

No — the team does thisไม่ต้อง ทีมโครงการดูแลเอง

The project team monitors overall Refer/Not-refer, sex, and left/right coverage. Clinicians do not need to manage dataset quotas themselves.

No counting, no forms, no grading before review. If more of one kind is needed, the coordinator will say so for the whole batch.
